**Where:** the Calendar, hover any post card

## The card actions

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Duplicate** | Opens a copy in the composer, ready to place elsewhere |
| **Preview** | Opens the post's public page, see [Sharing for approval](/general/approvals) |
| **Statistics** | Per-post performance and short link clicks |
| **Delete** | Removes the post |

## Deleting

**Delete** on a calendar card removes that channel's post, with its thread
items or comments. A post scheduled to five channels shows as five cards, and
the other four stay.

To remove it everywhere, click the card and use **Delete Post** in the
composer. When the post was created for more than one channel, Postiz asks:

| Choice | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Delete from all channels** | Removes the post from every channel in the composer |
| **Only from** *channel* | Removes it only from the channel you are viewing |

## Statistics

The Statistics modal shows how a published post performed, over 7, 30 or 90
days depending on what the platform supports.

Below the metrics is the **short link** table: each shortened URL in the post,
the destination, and how many clicks it got. This only has rows if the post was
saved with short links enabled, see
[Links and validation](/general/composer/links-and-validation).

<Note>
Not every platform reports per-post metrics. See
[Analytics](/general/analytics#per-post-metrics) for which do.
</Note>

## Connecting a post Postiz lost track of

Occasionally Postiz publishes a post but never receives the platform's ID for
it, usually because the platform timed out after accepting the content. The
post shows as published but has no statistics and no link to the live version.

The Statistics modal then offers a **connect** step: you paste the live post's
URL or pick it from the recent posts the platform reports, and Postiz
reattaches the two. Statistics start working from that point.

The same operation exists in the API, see
[Update release ID](/public-api/posts/update-release-id).

## Debugging a failed post

A post with a red ring has the platform's own error message in its tooltip.
That message is the thing to search for or to quote when asking for help, since
it comes from the platform rather than from Postiz.
