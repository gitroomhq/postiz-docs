**Where:** the Calendar section, the app's home screen

The calendar is both the plan and the record: scheduled posts, drafts and
published posts all sit on the same grid.

## Views

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Day** | Rows built from your channels' time slots, plus any time that already has a post |
| **Week** | Seven columns, 24 hourly rows |
| **Month** | A six by seven grid. Clicking a day opens the composer on that day |
| **List** | Posts grouped by date, paginated, with state filters |

Day view is the one worth understanding. Its rows are not every hour, they are
your [time slots](/guide/channels/time-slots), so the day reads as a plan
rather than a wall of empty hours.

List view swaps the view switcher for state filters: **All**, **Scheduled**,
**Draft**, **Published**, 100 posts per page. It is the fastest way to answer
"what is still a draft".

Your chosen view is remembered between sessions, and it is in the URL, so you
can bookmark or share a specific week.

## Filtering by client

When you have more than one [customer](/guide/channels/customers), a customer
selector appears in the toolbar. Picking one filters the calendar to that
client's channels only.

## Reading a post card

- **Colour** comes from the post's first [tag](/guide/composer/scheduling).
- **`Draft:`** prefix means it will never publish.
- **A red ring** means it failed. The error is in the tooltip.
- **Struck-through and unclickable** means the slot is in the past.
- **"+ Show more (N)"** appears when a cell holds more than three posts.

## Creating a post from the calendar

Click any empty slot and the composer opens with that time filled in. Past
slots are not clickable.

If you have [sets](/guide/settings/reusables), Postiz asks whether to start
from one before opening the composer.

## Post actions

Hover a post card for **Duplicate**, **Preview**, **Statistics** and
**Delete**. See [Post actions and statistics](/guide/posts/actions-and-stats).

## The channel sidebar

The left sidebar lists your channels, grouped by customer where you have set
that up. From here you add channels, open the channel menu, create a post, and
reach the AI post generator.

Group headings collapse, and the whole sidebar collapses, which is worth doing
once you have a lot of channels.
