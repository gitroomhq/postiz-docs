**Where:** Calendar, channel `...` menu, **Move / add to group**

If you run social for more than one client, grouping channels by client is what
makes the calendar readable. Postiz calls these groups **customers**.

## Grouping channels

Assign a channel to a customer from its menu, or drag the channel onto a group
in the sidebar. A channel belongs to at most one customer, and **Remove from
customer** puts it back in the ungrouped list.

Once channels are grouped, the sidebar shows them under collapsible group
headings, and a **customer selector** appears in the calendar toolbar. It only
appears when more than one customer exists.

## What grouping buys you

- **Filter the whole calendar** to one client. Everything else disappears, so
  you can review one client's week without noise.
- **Select all of a client's channels at once** in the composer. Picking the
  customer selects their channels in one action.
- **Keep clients visually separate** in a sidebar that would otherwise be a
  long flat list of avatars.

## Invite links

You do not need a client's password to manage their accounts. Next to **Add
Channel** there is a second button that generates an **invite link**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate the link">
    Click the invite button in the channel sidebar and choose the platform the
    client should connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it to the client">
    They open the link, authorise on the platform's own screen, and never see
    your Postiz account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The channel appears in your organisation">
    Assign it to their customer group and start scheduling.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Invite links only work for platforms that connect through a clean redirect.
Platforms needing an instance URL, credentials typed into Postiz, or the
browser extension are not offered, because those all require someone to enter
values inside your Postiz instance.
</Note>

## Approval workflow

Grouping pairs with the [preview and approval page](/guide/approvals): group a
client's channels, draft their week, then send preview links for sign-off
before anything publishes.
