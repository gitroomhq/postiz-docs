title: 'Connect Channel (OAuth)'
openapi: 'GET /social/{integration}'
icon: 'plug'
---

The returned URL is the platform's own authorization page, so it suits connecting an account you are signed in to yourself. To hand the step to someone else, use [Connect Channel (Link)](/public-api/integrations/connect-link), which first shows them a Postiz page explaining what they are connecting.
